# NPLM -- Under Construction

I have spent a good amount of time working on NPLM's or Neural Probabilistic Language Models
the predecessors to the modern large language model and a fascinating facet of the history
of machine learning models that learn about language. The predecessor to the NPLM was 
n-grams, which are quite simple, count the number of 
occurences of a given sequence of n words over the total number of sequences of n-length that there are. The formula for this is quite simple. ** Lei Mao has a great writeup of the
derivation for the MLE [here](https://leimao.github.io/blog/Maximum-Likelihood-Estimation-Ngram/).**

p(w_0, w_1, w_2, .., w_n) \approx p(w_0) p(w_1 \vert w_0)


I rewrote this model from scratch in torch [here](https://github.com/AndrewLutsky/NPLM.git) if
you are interested in checking it out.
